### What this is

Lug to Lug is a personal software project by Vinayak Mokashi: a watch try-on that shows any watch on a photo of your wrist at its true size. To prove that its sizing is accurate, it needs photos of real wrists wearing watches of known size. This form asks for your permission to take and use those photos.

### What taking part involves

- **Photos of your wrist and forearm** (your hand may be in them) wearing a series of watches, taken from above with a phone on a stand. Your face is not photographed. A session takes about 15 to 30 minutes.
- **Measurements of your wrist:** the circumference with a soft tape, and the width of your wrist and hand with calipers.
- **Your skin tone as a group** on the Monk Skin Tone scale, where you choose the closest swatch. It is used only to check that the app works equally well for everyone, and results are reported for groups, never for one person.

You can cover tattoos or other marks if you prefer, and you can stop at any point.

**Your choice:**

- Photos of your wrist may be published (cropped to your wrist and the watch), in the public benchmark and on the project's pages:  [ ] Yes  [ ] No

If you leave a choice blank, the answer is **No**.

### How your data is kept

- Everywhere in the project you are identified only by your participant ID. Your name appears only on this form, which is kept privately.
- Your photos are stored privately, outside the public code repository. They are processed only for this project, on the cloud services it uses (for example Google Cloud, Modal, Kaggle and AI model APIs), and are never sold or shared for any other purpose.
- Location data is removed from every photo.
- Your data is kept until the project ends or until you withdraw, whichever comes first, and is then deleted.

### Withdrawing

You can withdraw at any time without giving a reason, by telling Vinayak. Within 14 days your photos and measurements are deleted from private storage, and they are left out of every later release of the benchmark. Anything published with your permission is taken down from the project's own pages, but copies other people have already made cannot be recalled. Your photos will not be used for any new training; a model already trained with them is used only until its next update.
