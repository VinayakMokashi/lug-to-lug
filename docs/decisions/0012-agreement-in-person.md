# ADR-0012: Volunteers agree in person, without signed forms

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-01

## Context

[ADR-0006](0006-data-consent-and-privacy.md) asked for written consent from every person before their first photo, and a form was written for it. The people in the benchmark are the builder and three volunteers who know the builder personally and take part in person. For them a signed form adds paperwork, and it was never going to change how their data is handled: the privacy rules protect them either way.

The project's public documents should describe what actually happens, so they can't go on saying "written consent" or "signed by every participant" once no form is signed.

## Decision

This replaces the "Consent first" rule of ADR-0006. Everything else in that record stands.

- **Agreement comes first, in person.** Before anyone's first photo or measurement, the builder tells them what taking part involves and how their data is used and kept ([participant information](../data/participant-information.md)), and they agree in person. Only adults take part.
- **No form is signed, and no record of the agreement is kept.** The only notes are of exceptions: someone keeping their photos out of training, saying yes to publishing an image, or withdrawing.
- **One agreement covers evaluation and training.** A person who would rather stay out of training says so, and their photos are used for evaluation only.
- **An image of a person is published only if they say yes.** Saying nothing means no.
- **Withdrawal works as before:** tell the builder, and within 14 days their data is deleted and left out of every later release.

## Consequences

- The README, plan, capture protocol, data card and project page describe agreement in person. The consent form becomes the participant information, with nothing to tick or sign.
- There is no document to show that a person agreed. The data card says so.
- This rests on every participant knowing the builder. Taking part from anyone else needs a new decision first.

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep the signed forms:** a stronger record, and the usual practice for a benchmark of people. The builder decided it isn't needed between friends.
- **Stop mentioning consent:** no false claim, but readers of a benchmark of people's wrists should be able to see how it was gathered.
